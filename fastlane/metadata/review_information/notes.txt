Three behaviours are deliberate. A short pass can treat them as gaps.

1. After "Skip" or "Continue" on a first-time device with nothing saved, "Discover", "Ash", "Profile" and "Settings" are missing. The headline is "Nothing is on the board." and the only control is "Stash a title". That empty-board screen is intended. One stash, or any later burn, brings back "Burn the gate" and those four controls. The full-page empty state does not return unless "Reset all data" clears the board.

2. "Run onboarding again" in "Settings" closes the sheet and shows the three-page cover again on purpose. The stack, the gate and the burns stay. "Skip" on any page, or "Continue" on page 3, returns to the board as it was left.

3. After the last seated title is burned and the stack is empty, the board stays up. The chip reads "Open" and the lane shows "No title is seated." It does not return to "Nothing is on the board." because burns now exist. Use "Stash" for the next title.
